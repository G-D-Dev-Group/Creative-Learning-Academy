# Mathematics (TEA Chapter 111)

STAAR-tested: grades 3-8, plus the Algebra I end-of-course exam.

Every grade also includes **Mathematical Process Standards** (problem-solving, using tools, multiple representations, communication, justification) -- a constant thread not repeated below for each grade, but present throughout.

## Kindergarten
- **Number & Operations:** count 0-20, compose/decompose numbers to 10
- **Addition/Subtraction:** model joining/separating, sums to 10 with objects
- **Geometry & Measurement:** identify 2D shapes/3D solids, compare length/weight
- **Data Analysis:** sort into categories, picture graphs
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** income sources, wants vs. needs

## Grade 1
- **Number & Operations:** compose/decompose to 120, place value comparisons
- **Addition/Subtraction:** sums/differences within 20
- **Algebraic Reasoning:** skip-count by 2s/5s/10s, equal sign as relationship
- **Geometry & Measurement:** classify shapes, halves/fourths, time to the half-hour
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** income, spending vs. saving

## Grade 2
- **Number & Operations:** compose/decompose to 1,200, number lines
- **Fractions (new):** halves/fourths/eighths
- **Addition/Subtraction:** automatic recall within 20, multi-step to 1,000
- **Geometry & Measurement:** area via square units, time to the nearest minute
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** savings, deposits/withdrawals, producers vs. consumers

## Grade 3 STAAR-tested
- **Number & Operations:** place value to 100,000
- **Fractions:** denominators 2/3/4/6/8, equivalence and comparison
- **Computation:** fluent addition/subtraction to 1,000, multiplication facts to 10x10
- **Geometry & Measurement:** quadrilateral classification, area via multiplication
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** labor/income link, scarcity, credit basics

## Grade 4 STAAR-tested
- **Number & Operations:** place value to 1 billion, decimals to hundredths
- **Fractions:** unlike-denominator comparison, addition with like denominators
- **Computation:** standard algorithms for all four operations
- **Geometry & Measurement:** angles, protractor use, symmetry
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** fixed vs. variable expenses, profit

## Grade 5 STAAR-tested
- **Number & Operations:** decimals to thousandths
- **Computation:** fluent multi-digit multiplication/division; fraction and decimal operations (capstone of elementary arithmetic)
- **Algebraic Reasoning:** numerical patterns, order of operations, volume formulas
- **Geometry & Measurement:** coordinate plane introduced, ordered pairs
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** tax types, gross vs. net income, budgeting

## Grade 6 STAAR-tested
- **Number & Operations:** fluent operations across positive and negative rational numbers
- **Proportionality (new strand):** ratios, rates, percents, unit conversion
- **Expressions, Equations & Relationships:** equivalent expressions, equations for geometric formulas
- **Measurement & Data:** full four-quadrant coordinate plane, measures of center/spread
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** checking accounts, debit cards, credit history

## Grade 7 STAAR-tested
- **Proportionality:** constant rate of change, similarity, probability
- **Expressions, Equations & Relationships:** linear relationships across representations, two-step equations/inequalities
- **Measurement & Data:** dot plots/box plots, random sampling
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** sales/income tax, net worth, simple/compound interest

## Grade 8 STAAR-tested -- algebra-readiness capstone
- **Number & Operations:** real numbers including irrationals, scientific notation
- **Proportionality:** slope as constant rate of change, functions foundation
- **Expressions, Equations & Relationships:** Pythagorean Theorem, equations with variables on both sides, simultaneous linear equations
- **2D Shapes:** transformations (rotation, reflection, translation, dilation)
- **Personal Financial Literacy:** loan comparison, investment growth, college cost planning

## Algebra I STAAR EOC-tested
Linear functions/equations/inequalities and systems; quadratic functions and equations (graphing, factoring); exponential functions (growth/decay); polynomial operations, radicals, exponent laws, factoring.

## Geometry
Coordinate and transformational geometry; logical argument and formal constructions; multi-format proofs (triangles, angles, quadrilaterals); similarity and trigonometry; area/volume and dimensional change; circle theorems; probability (permutations, combinations, conditional probability).

## Algebra II
Function families and inverses (square root, rational, exponential, logarithmic); systems including quadratics; transformations and asymptotic behavior; complex numbers, polynomial factoring, rational expressions; regression modeling.

## Precalculus
Function composition and inverses; parametric/polar equations, conic sections; unit circle, Law of Sines/Cosines, vectors; series, sequences, the Binomial Theorem, trig identities.

## Other high school options
**Algebraic Reasoning** -- an alternate track covering function classification, matrices, and modeling from real data. **Statistics** -- a one-credit course on study design, data description, distributions, and inference. **Advanced Quantitative Reasoning** -- a capstone applying statistics/modeling to real-world data. **AP/IB courses** (Statistics, Precalculus, Calculus AB/BC, Math Analysis & Approaches, Math Applications & Interpretations) follow College Board/IB frameworks rather than separate TEA-authored strands.

## How this progresses, K-12
- **Number strand:** whole numbers (K-2) -> fractions/decimals (3-5) -> rational numbers (6-7) -> real numbers (8) -> complex numbers (Algebra II).
- **Algebraic thinking:** appears informally in Grade 1, formalizes into "Expressions, Equations & Relationships" and "Proportionality" in middle school, resolves into function-family-based high school courses.
- **Geometry:** shape identification (K-5) -> transformational geometry (grade 8) -> formal proof and trigonometry (Geometry course).
- **Data Analysis:** picture graphs (K) -> scaled bar graphs (3-4) -> scatterplots and sampling (7-8) -> regression (Algebra II) -> full inference (Statistics).
- **Personal Financial Literacy** appears every year K-8, scaling from wants/needs to loan amortization and college cost planning.
