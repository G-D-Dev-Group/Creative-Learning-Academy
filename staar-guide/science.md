# Science (TEA Chapter 112)

STAAR-tested: grade 5, grade 8, plus the Biology end-of-course exam.

Every grade shares five strand types: **Scientific and Engineering Practices** (process/inquiry skills), **Matter and Energy**, **Force, Motion, and Energy**, **Earth and Space**, and **Organisms and Environments**.

## Kindergarten
- **Practices:** ask questions, observe, use simple tools (hand lenses, thermometers)
- **Matter:** sort objects by shape, color, texture, material
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** object motion, magnetism, light sources
- **Earth & Space:** day/night and seasonal patterns, weather
- **Organisms:** plant needs, animal structures, plant life cycle

## Grade 1
- **Matter:** classify by shape/color/texture; predict heating/cooling effects
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** pushes/pulls change motion; heat in daily life
- **Earth & Space:** seasonal prediction, soil properties, water conservation
- **Organisms:** living vs. nonliving, simple food chains, animal life cycles

## Grade 2
- **Matter:** solids vs. liquids, changes from cutting/melting/freezing
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** contact forces, sound as vibrating matter
- **Earth & Space:** Sun as a star, Moon reflects sunlight, erosion
- **Organisms:** ecosystem relationships, food chains, pollination, metamorphosis

## Grade 3
- **Matter/Energy:** temperature, mass, states of matter, state changes
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** magnetism, gravity; light/sound/thermal/mechanical energy
- **Earth & Space:** Sun-Earth-Moon orbit models, planetary order; rapid surface changes (volcanoes, earthquakes)
- **Organisms:** migration/hibernation, energy flow in food chains, fossils as evidence

## Grade 4
- **Matter/Energy:** density, mixtures and solutions, conservation of matter
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** contact vs. at-a-distance forces; conductors/insulators, circuits
- **Earth & Space:** Moon-phase prediction, water cycle, weather vs. climate
- **Organisms:** photosynthesis basics, food webs, inherited vs. acquired traits

## Grade 5 STAAR-tested
- **Matter/Energy:** solubility, conductivity, particles too small to see
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** equal/unequal forces, energy transformations, circuits
- **Earth & Space:** Earth's rotation, sedimentary rock/fossil fuel formation
- **Organisms:** biotic/abiotic interactions, human impact on ecosystems, instinct vs. learned behavior

## Grade 6
- **Matter/Energy:** pure substances vs. mixtures, periodic table basics, density
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** net force, Newton's Third Law, potential vs. kinetic energy
- **Earth & Space:** Earth's revolution/seasons, Earth's spheres, rock cycle
- **Organisms:** predator/competitive/symbiotic relationships, cell theory, prokaryotic/eukaryotic cells

## Grade 7
- **Matter/Energy:** elements vs. compounds, physical vs. chemical change, solutions
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** speed vs. velocity, Newton's First Law, thermal energy transfer
- **Earth & Space:** solar system, plate tectonics evidence
- **Organisms:** trophic-level energy flow, human body systems, natural/artificial selection

## Grade 8 STAAR-tested
- **Matter/Energy:** chemical reactions, acids vs. bases, mass conservation (photosynthesis equations)
- **Force/Motion/Energy:** acceleration, all three Newton's Laws applied, wave characteristics, EM spectrum
- **Earth & Space:** stellar life cycles, galaxy classification, global weather circulation, climate-impact events
- **Organisms:** ecosystem stability/succession, cell organelles, chromosomes and inheritance

## Biology STAAR EOC-tested
Cell structure and biomolecules; cell cycle and DNA replication; genetics (DNA structure, PCR, meiosis, Punnett squares); evolution (fossil/molecular evidence, natural selection, speciation); photosynthesis/respiration; animal/plant system adaptations; ecosystem interdependence and biogeochemical cycles.

## Chemistry
Periodic table development and trends; atomic theory and electron configuration; bonding and nomenclature; moles and stoichiometry; kinetic molecular theory and gas laws; solutions and molarity; acids/bases; thermochemistry; nuclear chemistry.

## Physics
Kinematics and Newton's Laws; circuits and Coulomb's law; energy conservation and momentum; wave behavior and the electromagnetic spectrum; quantum phenomena (photoelectric effect, wave-particle duality).

## Integrated Physics and Chemistry (IPC)
For students not on the full Physics/Chemistry track -- force and motion basics, energy transfer, matter structure, atomic models, environmental impact of chemical/nuclear change.

## High school electives
**Aquatic Science** -- freshwater/marine ecosystems, watershed hydrology, human impacts. **Astronomy** -- history of astronomy, solar system formation, stellar life cycles, cosmology. **Earth Systems Science** -- Earth's four spheres, plate tectonics, climate science. **Environmental Systems** -- biomes, sustainability, population dynamics, human impact and policy.

## How this progresses, K-12
- **Matter:** observable properties (K) -> states/changes (2-3) -> mixtures/particle theory (4-5) -> atoms/elements (6-8) -> full atomic theory and stoichiometry (Chemistry).
- **Force/Motion:** pushes/pulls (K) -> basic forces (3) -> net force (6) -> all three Newton's Laws (8) -> vector kinematics and quantum phenomena (Physics).
- **Earth/Space:** day/night (K) -> orbit models (2-3) -> plate tectonics (7) -> cosmology intro (8) -> full cosmology and geologic time (Astronomy/Earth Systems Science).
- **Organisms:** plant/animal needs (K) -> food webs (2-4) -> cell theory and genetics basics (6-8) -> full genetics and evolution (Biology).
